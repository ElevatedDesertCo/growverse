> This base-building grow-sim design is preserved as a possible future direction or
> theming source for the MMO.

## One-Line Pitch
A mystical base-building grow simulator where Cultivators tend sacred cannabis in a cosmic desert realm, defend their growth, and rise through guilds to claim dominion over the Growverse.

## The Player Fantasy
You are a Cultivator — part botanist, part mystic — who has claimed a patch of the Growverse. Grow sacred plants, build a thriving base, defend it from raiders, raid others, rise through guilds. Vibe: mystical cosmic desert fantasy. NOT frat-bro stoner.

## Core Game Loop
1. Plants finish growing -> tap to harvest -> Leaf flows in
2. Spend Leaf to build or upgrade
3. New structure unlocks new strain, defense, or resource node
4. Plant new seeds -> repeat

## Resources
- Leaf (green): primary currency, from harvests
- Fire (orange): energy for raids and speedups, from Resin Distillery
- Mushroom (purple): premium currency, from quests and events

## Phase 1 Buildings (locked)
1. Grow Tent — grows cannabis on timer, produces Leaf
2. Drying Chamber — multiplies harvest yield
3. Resin Distillery — passively generates Fire
4. Sentinel Sprout — defensive plant (Phase 4 combat placeholder)

Each building has 5 upgrade tiers in Phase 1.

## Visual Direction
- Palette: deep desert ochre/brown, gold accents, sage green, warm orange, soft purple
- Style: illustrated, premium, card-based UI with golden borders
- Tone: mystical, calm, confident. No bro-stoner energy.

## Anti-Goals
- NOT frat-bro stoner
- NOT pay-to-win
- NOT real-time multiplayer (async only)
- NOT a Clash of Clans clone (borrows structure, not theme)

## Phased Roadmap
- Phase 1: Single-player core + PWA (4-6 weeks)
- Phase 2: Polish + share to friends (3-4 weeks)
- Phase 3: Accounts + cloud save via Supabase (4-6 weeks)
- Phase 4: Async raids + Circles (guilds) (8-12 weeks)
- Phase 5: Monetization + Elevated Desert Co. brand tie-ins
